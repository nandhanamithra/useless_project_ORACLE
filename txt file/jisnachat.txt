5/15/26, 12:21 AM - Mithra Nandhana: Common Market Entry Strategies
1. Exporting
Meaning

Produce in one place and sell in another.

Indian Example

Amul exports dairy products to international markets.

Advantage

Lower initial investment.

Disadvantage

Less control over overseas distribution.

2. Licensing
Meaning

Allow another company to use your technology or brand for a fee.

Example

A startup licenses a patented water filtration design.

Advantage

Fast expansion with minimal investment.

Disadvantage

Reduced control.

3. Franchising
Meaning

Others operate businesses using your brand and systems.

Indian Example

Chai Point and Wow! Momo have expanded using franchise-like models and partnerships.

Advantage

Rapid growth.

Disadvantage

Quality must be monitored carefully.

4. Subsidiary
Meaning

Set up and fully own a new business entity in another market.

Example

Infosys Limited establishing wholly owned overseas offices.

Advantage

Full control.

Disadvantage

High cost and complexity.

5. Joint Venture / Acquisition
Meaning

Partner with or purchase an existing company.

Indian Example

Walmart Inc. acquired a major stake in Flipkart to strengthen its presence in India.

Advantage

Immediate market access.

Disadvantage

Requires significant capital and integration effort.

6. MVP Launch (Startup Approach)
Meaning

Release a basic version of the product to test demand.

Example

A KTU-focused reminder app launches first in one college before expanding statewide.

Advantage

Low risk and rapid learning.
